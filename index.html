Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Quran Listener is a free app for listening to the Quran. It has no ads, no accounts and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your favourites, default reciter, listening position, downloaded recitations, and prayer-time settings (location, calculation method, madhab). These are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Location (only if you use prayer times)

If you turn on prayer times, the app needs your approximate location to calculate them. This works in one of two ways:

  • Your phone's location (if you choose "Use my current location"). Prayer times are calculated entirely on your phone — your location is never sent to us or to any server for this. It is used only in the calculation and then discarded.
  • A city you search for. Only the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's free geocoding service to find its coordinates — nothing that identifies you or your device.

Notifications (only if you turn on prayer time reminders)

If enabled, the app schedules local reminder notifications at each prayer time, entirely on your phone. This does not involve any server.

What the app connects to

To work, the app requests data over the internet from:

  • Quran Foundation — surah, Juz and verse information, translation, tafsir, and recitation audio links.
  • The servers Quran Foundation's audio links point to — the recitation files themselves.
  • Open-Meteo — only when you search for a city for prayer times, and only the city name you typed.
  • Google Fonts — fonts used for some text.

These services necessarily receive your device's IP address and the content you request, as with any website. We do not send them your name, email or any account, because none exists. Please see their own privacy policies.

Permissions this app requests, and why

  • Location (approximate/coarse) — only for prayer times, only when you turn that feature on, as described above.
  • Notifications — only for prayer time reminders, only when you turn that feature on.
  • Internet — to fetch Quran content and audio.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
